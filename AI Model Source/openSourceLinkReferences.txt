METHODS AND STANDARDS

  https://doi.org/10.1109/TBME.2016.2609282

  https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~lowe/425/violaJones01.pdf
- NEWS2: Royal College of Physicians, "National Early Warning Score (NEWS) 2", 2017
